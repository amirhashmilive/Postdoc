Ph.D. Dissertation Defense (2026)

A Comparative Study on Betterment of Society through Corporate Social Responsibility Communication in Chhattisgarh and Jharkhand

Author: Sayed Amir Mustafa Hashmi

Enrolment No.: R/Ph.D. 005

Institution: Kushabhau Thakre Patrakarita Avam Jansanchar Vishwavidyalaya, Raipur (C.G.)

Supervisor: Dr. Ashutosh Mandavi

Co-Supervisor: Dr. Nripendra Kumar Sharma

Temporal Scope: 5 Years (FY 2018-19 to FY 2022-23)

Executive Summary

Thesis Abstract

This doctoral dissertation investigates how corporate social responsibility (CSR) communication functions as a strategic driver for societal betterment in central India's primary industrial and mining states—Chhattisgarh and Jharkhand.

Utilizing a mixed-methods design comprising 400 beneficiary surveys (200 per state) and 40 stakeholder interviews (20 per state) across 10 districts over a five-year period (2018–2023), the study analyzes communication channels, linguistic accessibility, corporate transparency, and community perception across four core development sectors: education, healthcare, environment, and livelihood.

The empirical findings reveal a striking 26-point communication chasm in local language engagement between Chhattisgarh (80%) and Jharkhand (54%), establishing that verbal interpersonal communication outperforms printed materials by 3.4x in building community trust. To address governance deficits, the study introduces an original normative Five-Pillar Framework for sustainable CSR communication.

Methodological Rigor

Research Design

400 Beneficiaries

Structured quantitative surveys across 10 districts (200 Chhattisgarh, 200 Jharkhand).

40 Stakeholders

Qualitative interviews with CSR heads, government officers, NGO leaders, and village chiefs.

10 Districts

5 districts in Chhattisgarh (Raipur, Korba, Raigarh, Durg, Dantewada) vs 5 in Jharkhand (Ranchi, Dhanbad, East Singhbhum, Bokaro, West Singhbhum).

5 Financial Years

Longitudinal data evaluation covering FY 2018-19 through FY 2022-23.

Empirical Results

Key Findings

The Communication Chasm

26-Point Disparity: 80% of outreach in Chhattisgarh utilized local dialects (Chhattisgarhi/Halbi), compared to only 54% in Jharkhand (Santhali/Mundari), directly driving higher trust scores in Chhattisgarh (Score: 2.67 vs 2.14).

Channel Efficacy Hierarchy

Verbal channels (Gramsabhas, community facilitators, workshops) achieved a 78% retention rate compared to only 23% for printed pamphlets and billboards in tribal belts.

Sectoral Prioritization

Education (38%) and Healthcare (32%) received the highest communication visibility, whereas Environment (18%) and Livelihood (12%) suffered from acute information deficits.

State Performance Index

Chhattisgarh outperformed Jharkhand on 8 out of 9 empirical communication efficacy indicators due to institutionalized community consultation mechanisms.

Original Theoretical Model

Five-Pillar Framework

Pillar 1

Strategy

Aligning corporate CSR goals with local community development needs.

Pillar 2

Participation

Mandatory co-creation and Gramsabha dialogue in project formulation.

Pillar 3

Transparency

Vernacular disclosure of expenditure, timelines, and agency roles.

Pillar 4

Feedback

Institutionalized community grievance and suggestion channels.

Pillar 5

Impact

Independent socio-economic audit & societal betterment metrics.
