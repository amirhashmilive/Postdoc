Academic Background

Professional Biography

Dr. Sayed Amir Mustafa Hashmi

Dr. Sayed Amir Mustafa Hashmi is a communications researcher specializing in Strategic Communication, Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Communication, Sustainability Communication, and Development Communication.

He earned his Ph.D. in Mass Communication from Kushabhau Thakre University of Journalism and Mass Communication, Raipur. His research focuses on empirical evaluations of corporate social governance, indigenous language engagement, and ESG disclosure models in central India's industrial and mining belts.

Research Scope

  • Mixed-Methods Methodology: 400 surveys + 40 stakeholder interviews
  • Geographic Scope: Chhattisgarh & Jharkhand mining corridors
  • Key Framework: Original Five-Pillar CSR Governance Model
  • Target Sectors: Education, Healthcare, Environment, Livelihood

Research Philosophy

Philosophy

Communication is not merely an instrument for corporate reputation management; it is a fundamental pillar of democratic governance and social betterment. True sustainable development requires closing communication gaps between institutional decision-makers and grassroots beneficiaries through vernacular, participatory engagement.
