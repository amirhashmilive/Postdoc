Overview & Vision

Research Statement

Focus Areas

My research examines how strategic communication shapes corporate social responsibility, stakeholder trust, ESG disclosure, and community development outcomes — particularly in central India's tribal mining corridors.

Long-Term Vision

I develop evidence-based communication frameworks supporting sustainable corporate governance and inclusive development in emerging economies — bridging institutional disclosures with grassroots beneficiary understanding.

Doctoral Research Discovery

The Communication Chasm

26%

Language Gap Identified

80% local dialect use in Chhattisgarh vs only 54% in Jharkhand — a 26-point disparity that directly drives community trust and CSR awareness deficits.

3.4×

Verbal vs. Printed Media

Gramsabha community meetings outperform printed pamphlets by 3.4× in information retention among tribal beneficiaries across 10 studied districts.
