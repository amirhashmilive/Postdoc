Overview & Vision

Research Statement

My research examines how strategic communication influences corporate social responsibility, stakeholder engagement, sustainability, and community development. I employ mixed-methods research to investigate the effectiveness of CSR communication, ESG disclosure, public trust, and communication for social betterment.

My long-term research aims to develop evidence-based communication frameworks that support sustainable corporate governance and inclusive development across emerging economies and tribal industrial corridors.

Core Contributions

Research Highlights

26% Language Chasm

Identified a 26-point gap in local dialect usage between Chhattisgarh (80%) and Jharkhand (54%), explaining disparities in CSR awareness.

Five-Pillar Model

Formulated an original normative framework categorizing CSR communication into Strategy, Participation, Transparency, Feedback, and Impact.

Verbal vs Print Efficacy

Demonstrated that interpersonal and verbal community meetings outperform printed reports by 3.4x in tribal beneficiary trust.
