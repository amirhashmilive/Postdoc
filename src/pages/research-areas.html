Academic Specializations

Research Clusters

My scholarship spans five interconnected domains addressing how institutional communication drives social betterment, sustainability governance, and community trust.

📢

1. Strategic Communication

Corporate governance, stakeholder trust, crisis communication, and reputation management in industrial corridors.

🤝

2. CSR Communication

Beneficiary engagement models, mandatory vs voluntary disclosure, and grass-roots communication efficacy.

🌱

3. Sustainability Communication

ESG disclosure standards (GRI, SASB), greenwashing prevention, and environmental accountability.

Emerging Frontiers

Development & Technology

4. Development Communication (C4D)

Investigating participatory communication strategies in marginalized and tribal communities. Focused on overcoming linguistic barriers and evaluating corporate social investments in education, healthcare, and livelihood.

5. Emerging Technologies & AI

Analyzing the potential of generative AI, automated translation, and digital platforms to translate complex ESG disclosures into vernacular audio-visual media for indigenous beneficiaries.
