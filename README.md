# Postdoc Academic Portfolio

A professional academic portfolio website for **Sayed Amir Mustafa Hashmi**, researcher in Strategic Communication, Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR), Sustainability Communication, and Development Communication.

## 🌐 Live Site

[https://amirhashmilive.github.io/Postdoc/](https://amirhashmilive.github.io/Postdoc/)

## 🎓 About

This portfolio showcases research from a Ph.D. in Mass Communication at Kushabhau Thakre University of Journalism and Mass Communication, India. The thesis, *"A Comparative Study on Betterment of Society through Corporate Social Responsibility Communication in Chhattisgarh and Jharkhand"*, forms the research foundation.

## ✨ Features

- **13 Pages**: Home, About, Research Areas, Publications, PhD Research, Projects, Fellowships, Impact, Data & Resources, Teaching, News, Blog, Contact
- **Cinematic Glassmorphism Design**: Premium visual experience with glass cards, neon accents, and breathing glow animations
- **Dark/Light Theme**: Dual theme with localStorage persistence and smooth transitions
- **Publication System**: Search, filter, and export citations (BibTeX/APA)
- **Fellowship Tracker**: Interactive application status tracking
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-first with full-viewport scroll-snap presentation
- **Zero Dependencies**: Pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript

## 🛠 Technology Stack

| Technology | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| HTML5 | Semantic markup with accessibility |
